What to do, in order

Some of these run at the same time; some cannot start until another one lands. BPP will not register you without CAC, a Tax ID, PenCom and ITF already in hand, so it is always last — and starting there, as most people do, wastes the time you had.

What each certificate needs

Tap any certificate for the requirements, the law that imposes it, and the source. Where an agency publishes nothing, that is recorded as an absence — not filled in with a guess.

The six documents nobody counts

A federal tender demands twelve items, not six. The other half are not certificates, and they are where small bids usually die — audited accounts most of all.

Everything expires on the same day

These certificates do not run for a year from the day you get them. Almost all of them expire on 31 December regardless of issue date — PenCom states it outright, and federal tender adverts demand the rest “valid till 31st December”. So the whole country renews in January, and nobody is told.

What compliance actually costs

The certificate fees are not the barrier, and treating them as one repeats the very thing this tool exists to expose. To be issued a pension certificate you must show three fiscal years of remittance. That liability does not disappear because nobody told you about it — it accrues, with penalties, and it is what actually keeps small contractors out of public work.

This table is the point, not a feature

ComplyNG helps one contractor at a time. This record does something else: it states, with evidence and a date, whether six Nigerian federal institutions publish the requirements they enforce. That is a claim anyone can check, cite, contest, or act on — and it is the part that outlives any single bid.

The underlying dataset is open. Every requirement, fee, timeline, citation and recorded absence — including the official URLs that returned 404 — is in one machine-readable file, versioned in the repository, free to reuse.

The three that should not be possible

  • ITF — itf.gov.ng/itf-compliance/, the page that should carry the requirements for a certificate you are legally obliged to hold, currently serves Indonesian gambling affiliate spam. Its /about-us/ page returns 404. No archived copy exists.
  • BPP — the Public Procurement Act 2007, the Bureau's own governing statute, returns 404 on four separate BPP-hosted URLs. Its main FAQ page is unfinished placeholder text about managed IT services. "Interim Registration Report" appears nowhere on its site, though every federal tender demands that document by name.
  • NSITF — requires forms ECS RE01 and ECS RE03 to issue a certificate, and marks both as "Coming soon" on its own downloads page.
